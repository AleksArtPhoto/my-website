Booking and Cancellation Policy for Corporate Clients (B2B) ▾

Booking Confirmation & Deposit:
Your booking is provisionally held upon receiving the reservation request. To fully secure the time slot, a non-refundable deposit of 30% of the total service cost must be paid within 24 hours from the invoice issue date. If the payment is not received within this timeframe, the slot will be released automatically.

Cancellation Terms:

  • More than 7 days prior to the scheduled service: Cancellation is free of charge (excluding the non-refundable deposit).
  • Between 3 and 7 days prior: The Client shall pay 50% of the remaining balance of the total service cost.
  • Less than 48 hours prior or No-Show: The Client shall pay 100% of the remaining balance of the total service cost.

Travel & Expenses Reimbursement:
Any travel expenses, accommodation, or third-party bookings already incurred by the Provider specifically for this service are non-refundable and must be reimbursed by the Client in full within 7 business days upon presentation of receipts, regardless of when the cancellation occurs.
