I am a professional photographer and videographer based in Denmark (Zealand), working with people and brands who value natural, clean and authentic visual storytelling. My approach combines technical precision with relaxed direction, helping clients feel comfortable in front of the camera while creating modern Scandinavian-style visuals. I work with portraits, commercial projects, interiors and product photography, as well as video production for businesses and social media. My goal is simple — create visuals that feel professional, natural and timeless.

FAQ

How do I book a session?

You can book a session by clicking the "Book a photo session" button or contacting me directly via the contact form. We will then discuss the date, location, and all the details.

When will I receive my photos?

Standard delivery time is 7-14 days depending on the package. You will receive a link to a private online gallery where you can download your high-resolution images.

Do you provide RAW unedited files?

I do not provide RAW files. My work includes professional editing as part of the "Scandinavian style" experience, ensuring every photo meets high-quality standards.

Can we shoot at a specific location?

Absolutely! I’m open to your suggestions for locations, whether it's an outdoor spot, a studio, or your workspace for interior/commercial shoots.
